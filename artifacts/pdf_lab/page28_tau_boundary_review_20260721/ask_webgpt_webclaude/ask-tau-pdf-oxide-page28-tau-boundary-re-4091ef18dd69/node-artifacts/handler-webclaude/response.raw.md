A **QRA is a Question–Reasoning–Answer record bound to inspected evidence**, and the **reasoning — not just the final answer — is the auditable part**: a reviewer can see *why* the answer follows from the cited evidence, and challenge it. 193: 194: - **Candidate QRAs are not authoritative.** Generated QRAs must pass grounding gates or stay `NEEDS_REVIEW`; being visible is not being approved. 195: - **Human-reviewed QRAs are intended to provide reusable, evidence-grounded interpretations** for later questions — so a settled question does not need to be re-reasoned from scratch, and the interpretation stays traceable to its evidence.
